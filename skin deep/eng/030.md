Trevor, the Chosen, and Connie, a Werewolf, both survived a vampire attack the other night, and they both blame each other for Laura ending up in the hospital as a result. When Connie makes a snide remark about Trevor being too weak to protect his friends, Trevor Lashes Out at her and she hits back harder. Connie was already at 2 Harm after her leg got caught in a bear trap, but the group decides that doesn’t have much to do with being punched in the face for 1 Harm—and Trevor’s just trying to make a point. She stays at 2 Harm and takes the Condition bloody nose. Trevor took 1 Harm last night and has a bad bruise on his ribs, but Connie’s too angry to take advantage of it. She wails on him hard, but stops herself before sending another friend of hers to the hospital. Connie’s hit is worth 2 Harm, but instead of going to 3, Trevor moves up to a total of 2 Harm. He also gets the condition sore spot to reflect his older, less-important injury—and his short temper right now. Monique decides to try and hold a pillow over Laura’s face, an attack that would only be worth 1 Harm at best against someone in a position to defend themselves, but Monique is definitely taking advantage of Laura’s injuries.
